# Uniora

Uniora is a university research and administration platform for institutions that need a clear, dependable way to manage academic records, people, documents, workflows and day-to-day operations.

It brings the work of university teams into one place while keeping each institution's information separated and carefully controlled.

## What Uniora helps with

- Student, professor and academic records
- Research projects, workshops and review workflows
- Official documents, certificates and printable records
- Calendars, reports, exports and operational follow-up
- Student and professor portals
- Administrative work, approvals, audit history and support operations
- Background processing for reliable long-running tasks

## Who it is for

Uniora is designed for universities, faculties, research offices and academic administration teams. It supports the people who maintain institutional records as well as the students, professors and reviewers who need a straightforward view of their work.

## Product principles

- Every institution sees only the information that belongs to it.
- Important actions leave a trace that can be reviewed later.
- Official records are preserved and versioned instead of silently overwritten.
- Sensitive operations require explicit authorization.
- External services and unfinished operations fail safely rather than pretending to succeed.
